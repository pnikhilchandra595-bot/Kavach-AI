# Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL)
## Standard Operating Procedure: Atmospheric Distillation Column Startup & Pre-Heating Protocol
**Document Code:** MRPL-SOP-CRU-101  
**Revision:** 5.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-20  
**Refinery Unit:** CDU-II  
**Target System:** Atmospheric Fractionation Train (Columns C-101 / C-102)

---

### Section 1: Pre-Startup Lineup & Blind De-Isolation Checklist
1. Verify that all spectacle blinds installed for turnaround isolation on crude feed line 24"-CRU-101 have been reversed to open position and witnessed by Operations Engineer.
2. Confirm hydrostatic or pneumatic leak test completion at 1.10 times operating pressure with zero decay over 60 minutes.
3. Establish positive nitrogen blanket throughout Column C-101 at 0.5 bar(g). Oxygen content inside column vapor spaces must not exceed 0.5% vol before introducing hydrocarbons.

### Section 2: Hot Oil Circulation & Reflux Drum De-Watering
1. Initiate hot gas oil circulation through boot coolers to warm shell courses gradually. Maximum allowable warming rate is 25 °C/hour to avoid differential thermal stress on clad plates.
2. Monitor accumulator boot level transmitter LT-104. Any accumulated sour water must be manually drained to the closed blowdown sewer before top reflux pump P-102A/B startup.

### Section 3: Furnace Light-off & Temperature Control
1. Light pilot burners in Crude Furnace F-101 strictly following NFPA 85 flame safeguard sequence.
2. Maintain transfer line temperature ramp at 30 °C/hr until flash zone temperature reaches 360 °C.
3. In case of sudden flameout, immediately trip emergency shutoff valve ESDV-1001 and initiate steam purging of the radiant firebox for at least 15 minutes before attempting restart.
