# Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL)
## Standard Operating Procedure: Hydrocracker Reactor High-Pressure Safeguarding
**Document Code:** MRPL-SOP-HYC-205  
**Revision:** 3.1  
**Effective Date:** 2025-10-12  
**Refinery Unit:** Hydrocracker Unit (HCU)  
**Target Equipment:** Reactors R-201A / R-201B (2.25Cr-1Mo-V Steel)

---

### Section 1: Operating Limits & Temperature Exotherm Quench
1. Operating hydrogen partial pressure must be maintained at $\ge 145.0\text{ bar(g)}$ with purity $> 99.2\%$.
2. Reactor bed differential temperatures ($\Delta T_{bed}$) must be monitored continuously across 32 multi-point thermocouples (TI-201 through TI-232).
3. If any catalyst bed exotherm rate exceeds **2.5 °C/minute**, the automatic hydrogen quench control valve (HCV-2012) must be engaged to restore thermal equilibrium.

### Section 2: Emergency Depressurization System (EDP)
1. In the event of an unmanageable temperature runaway exceeding 455 °C, the SIL-3 Emergency Depressurization System (EDP) must be activated immediately.
2. The high-pressure loop shall depressurize to 20 bar(g) within 15 minutes at a rate not exceeding 10.0 bar/minute to protect against hydrogen embrittlement and catastrophic acoustic vibration.
3. Effluent from EDP must vent directly to the High-Pressure Acid Flare Knockout Drum (V-701) with emergency steam assist active.
