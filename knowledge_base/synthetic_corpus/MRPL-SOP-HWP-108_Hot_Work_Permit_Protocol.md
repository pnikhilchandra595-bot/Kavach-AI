# Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL)
## Standard Operating Procedure: Hazardous Hot Work Permit & Toxic Gas Isolation
**Document Code:** MRPL-SOP-HWP-108  
**Revision:** 6.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-10  
**Refinery Unit:** Refinery Wide / HSE Department  
**Governing Standard:** OISD-STD-105 / MRPL Safety Manual Chapter 4

---

### Section 1: Permit Classification & Jurisdiction
1. **Class-A Hot Work Permit (Naked Flame):** Mandatory for welding, burning, torch cutting, open grinding, and non-explosion-proof electrical tool usage within battery limits of operating hydrocarbon units.
2. Validity: Valid for a single 8-hour shift only. Requires physical re-inspection and gas testing signature at every shift change.

### Section 2: Mandatory Atmospheric Gas Testing Limits
Prior to striking an arc or starting any spark-producing machinery:
1. **Lower Explosive Limit (LEL):** Must read **0.0% LEL** on a freshly bump-tested multi-gas detector. Any reading $> 1.0\%$ mandates immediate work stoppage.
2. **Hydrogen Sulfide ($H_2S$):** Must be **$< 5.0\text{ ppm}$** (MRPL refinery 8-hr TWA threshold). If $> 10\text{ ppm}$, SCBA positive-pressure respirators are strictly mandatory.
3. **Oxygen Content ($O_2$):** Must be strictly between **19.5% and 21.0%** by volume.
4. Continuous gas monitoring with audible alarms must remain active within a 3.0-meter radius of the hot work activity for the entire duration.

### Section 3: Isolation Requirements & Fire Watch
1. All process lines within 15 meters containing flammable vapors must be positively blinded with rated slip spades; valved isolation alone is strictly unacceptable.
2. A dedicated, trained Fire Watch equipped with a pressurized 50 kg Dry Chemical Powder (DCP) trolley and charged fire hose connected to refinery hydrant ring main must remain present throughout and for 30 minutes after completion.
